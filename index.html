The execution interlock

One run, every boundary.

Follow the solid numbered rail from an inbound message to its delivery receipt. Dashed rails show side authority: settings, secrets, human review, and recovery. The model never owns durable state, credentials, permission settlement, or delivery.

Durability precedes side effects.

Gantry commits the canonical message and admission row before a worker may call a model or tool. NOTIFY improves latency; it does not become the source of truth.

The runner is powerful, not authoritative.

It receives a bounded projection and short-lived gateway token. Host-owned secrets, reviewed capabilities, and settlements stay outside the child process.

Completion has two dimensions.

Generation can complete while delivery is partial or failed. Gantry persists text, delivery items, receipts, and the fenced run outcome separately.

Trust model

Four things the model cannot grant itself.

Gantry separates identity, durable ownership, selected authority, and execution containment. A persuasive prompt or successful classifier cannot collapse those boundaries.

Identity

Who and where?

App ownership, provider account, conversation route, sender policy, thread identity, installed agent, and control approvers are resolved independently.

Ownership

Which generation?

Capacity slots, unique live scope, lease token, worker identity, and fencing version decide which process may act and settle.

Authority

Which exact action?

Versioned semantic capabilities and granular rules are durable authority. Inventory, prompts, skill text, MCP metadata, and classifier output are not.

Containment

Inside which boundary?

Protected paths, signed IPC, outer sandboxing, egress policy, credential brokering, and bounded artifacts constrain the execution surface.

Failure semantics

Crashes become state transitions.

Gantry’s recovery story is not “retry everything.” It preserves idempotency, checks ownership, raises the fence, and records when delivery or authority remains unresolved.

A

Notification is missed

The durable admission row remains due; the scanner claims it later.

B

Worker loses its lease

A recovery owner takes a higher fencing generation; stale settlement fails.

C

Follow-up reaches another worker

It appends a sequenced command to the durable owner inbox.

D

Approval crosses restart

The pending interaction survives and resolves into the current owner’s fence.

E

Provider delivery is partial

The sent prefix and retry tail settle separately; duplicates stay bounded.

F

Settings projection fails

The worker retains last-good state and stays unready until forward correction.

G

Embedding provider is unavailable

Turn-time memory falls back to lexical recall; backfill can resume later.

H

Tool policy cannot decide safely

Classifier or metadata failure asks or denies—it does not silently allow.
